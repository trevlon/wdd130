Stand up paddle boarding offers, is a more laid back relaxing water experience. It is perfect for all ages, moving along still waters, enaging your body for a workout.

We don't only do tours in the states, you can book trips from all over the world. Europe, Italy, Caribbean etc.

Join us our snorkeling tour in the coral reefs. A truely beautiful underwater adventure. As you go below the water you will enter into the world of marine life.

It's a month long trip where we will be going to The Great Barrier Reef, The Red Sea, and finally the Hawaiian Islands waters. Booking ends on September 25th, 2026.

We have a water rafting club called Water Ways, if you are interested you can become a member. Get firsthand updates on trips, tournaments and tours.

There is a training period, where you would be trained, and possibly join our sports rafting team and compete. Contact us @ info@whitewater.com.
