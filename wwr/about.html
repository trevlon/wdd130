History

White Water Rafting opened in 2008, and is up and running still. It began to grew in popularity around 2010. Our founder Gavin Andrews came up with the idea of starting this company through his own rafting experience with his family as a child. His dad use to take him on solo rafting adventures when they went camping.

As an adult, he reflected upon the good times he had with his dad rafting, and wanted others to experience that joy he had as well. So he decided to start a water rafting company in Calorado. When it grew in popularity he then expanded it to world tours, and added other activiies like, snorkeling and stand up paddle boarding.

Adventure Awaits You!

A water rafting trip is a guided tour down a river in an inflatable raft, offering a mix of adventure and scenic beauty.

The trip can vary in difficulty, from calm and relaxing to thrilling and challenging, depending on the river conditions.

It is a great way to bond with friends or family, as participants work together to navigate the river.

The trip involves wearing wet clothes, sunscreen, and following the instructions of the rafting guide. raftingescapes.com
