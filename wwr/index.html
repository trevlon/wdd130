Our Purpose

Our purpose is to provide an exciting and fun experience using water rafting for families, friends and individuals.

These will be guided rafting trips with professionals to ensure your safety. Come join us, and have a life-altering adventure.

What we are offering?

The day time rafting tour is from everyone, and is usually about a two and a half hour tour, we open at 8am and close off at 6 to prepare for the night tour.

The night time rafting trips are for adults only, its a two hour rafting tour, from 7pm to 9pm. The moon and the stars a visible at this time, where their light reflect on the waters below.
